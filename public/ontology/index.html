Moon's extension of BIBFRAME

امتداد مون لـ BIBFRAME

The few terms Moon uses beside BIBFRAME 3.0.1 and BFLC, at https://mooon.site/ontology/. As data: Turtle (OWL and SKOS, CC0). See also Moon's SKOS vocabularies, maqām and copy actions; BIBFRAME in Arabic; Arabic genre and form terms; open metadata; the catalogue.

Hijri dates are kept as written, beside their EDTF form (http://id.loc.gov/datatypes/edtf).

moonext:Expression · Expression · تعبير
class · a kind of bf:Work

A Work as one realization of a Hub (a translation, an edition of the text): what BIBFRAME 3 folds into bf:Work, kept apart where Moon's editor distinguishes it. Published as bf:Work too.

moonext:AccessibilityContent · Accessibility content · محتوى الإتاحة لذوي الإعاقة
class

What makes a resource accessible (captions, audio description, large print). BIBFRAME 3.0.1 has the property but Moon keeps its value as its own class.

moonext:accessibilityContent · accessibility content · محتوى الإتاحة
object property · to moonext:AccessibilityContent

The accessibility content of a resource, as Moon's editor records it.

moonext:GovernmentPublication · Government publication · منشور حكومي
class

The kind of government body that issued a resource (national, local, international).

moonext:governmentPublication · government publication · منشور حكومي
object property · to moonext:GovernmentPublication

That a resource was issued by a government body, and which kind.

moonext:projectedProvisionDate · projected publication date · تاريخ النشر المتوقع
datatype property · on bf:Instance

When a resource not yet published is expected to appear (as bflc:projectedProvisionDate).

moonext:TransmissionEvent · Transmission event · حدث نقل
class · a kind of bf:Event · in the editor, not yet published

An event recorded in a manuscript about how its text was taught, read, copied or held: an ijāzah, a samāʿ, a reading, a colophon, an ownership or a waqf note. A bf:Event (plan step F2), with its people as bf:Contribution, its place as bf:place and its date as bf:date (the Hijri date as written, and EDTF).

moonext:participant · participant · مشارك
object property · on moonext:TransmissionEvent · to bf:Contribution · in the editor, not yet published

A person in the event, with the role as an LC relator (tch teacher, scr scribe, fmo former owner, dnr donor, wit witness, asn for the roles LC has no term for) and the role in the event (moonext:roleInEvent).

moonext:roleInEvent · role in the event · الدور في الحدث
datatype property · in the editor, not yet published

The person's role as the manuscript says it: teacher (الشيخ (المُجيز / المُسمِع)), student (الطالب (المُجاز)), reader (القارئ), listener (السامع), scribe (الناسخ), owner (المالك), endower (الواقف), beneficiary (الموقوف عليه), witness (الشاهد), annotator (صاحب التعليق).
